Privacy Policy

Last updated July 25, 2026

Mingo is a social app that helps people meet and connect. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information We Collect

When you use Mingo, we may collect the following:

  • Phone number — to verify your identity when you sign in
  • Profile information — your name, age, bio, and photos you choose to share
  • Messages — text conversations you have with your matches
  • Usage data — how you interact with the app, such as features used and time spent

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To show your profile to other users
  • To enable messaging between matched users
  • To improve the app experience and fix issues
  • To send important notifications about your account

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase, which provides industry-standard encryption and security measures. We do not sell your personal information to any third party.

Data Sharing

We do not share your personal data with third parties, except:

  • When required by law or legal process
  • With service providers that help us operate the app, such as Firebase for secure data storage and phone verification

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • View and edit your profile information at any time within the app
  • Delete your account and all associated data by contacting us
  • Request a copy of your personal data

Children's Privacy

Mingo is not intended for users under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated through the app.
